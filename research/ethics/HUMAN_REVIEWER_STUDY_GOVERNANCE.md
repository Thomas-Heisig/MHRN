# Human reviewer study governance

**Status:** mandatory pre-execution governance for RQ-EPIST-002, RQ-ETH-001 and any
future reviewer/coder study. This file does not constitute ethics approval.

Before recruiting or collecting human responses, the responsible researcher must freeze:

- study purpose, participant population and inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- whether institutional ethics review is required and the recorded decision/reference;
- informed-consent text and withdrawal procedure;
- data minimisation rules and a pseudonymous participant identifier scheme;
- categories of personal data collected;
- lawful basis / applicable privacy basis for the actual study context;
- retention period and deletion procedure;
- storage location, access roles and export rules;
- compensation, if any;
- confidentiality requirements for unpublished MHRN material;
- handling of AI tools by reviewers, including disclosure and confidentiality;
- contact route for participant questions and data-rights requests.

No email address, IP address, account handle or free-text identity field belongs in the
scientific DATA unless the frozen protocol establishes a necessary purpose.

## Data separation

Consent/identity records, if needed, must be stored separately from scientific responses.
Published datasets must be de-identified and reviewed for re-identification risk.

## Execution gate

`execution_authorized=false` remains mandatory until the study-specific ethics/privacy
record is complete. Repository implementation work never changes that flag automatically.
