# Human reviewer study governance

Status: mandatory pre-execution contract; not ethics approval.

Before any human reviewer/coder study starts, freeze participant information and consent, data minimisation, pseudonymisation or justification, retention/deletion period, access roles, withdrawal handling, compensation/conflicts where applicable, ethics/IRB applicability decision, and incident/correction procedure.

Repository automation may not mark these approved on behalf of a person or institution. Human participation remains blocked until the responsible human records the required decisions.
